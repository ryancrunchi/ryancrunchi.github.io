## Support development

CyComp is free. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. If the app helps your rides, you can leave a tip on [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/ryancrunchi). That page is outside the app.

## Pairing sensors

Turn the sensor on, open CyComp, and add a heart rate, power, speed, or cadence widget. The app scans for Bluetooth cycling sensors (Heart Rate, Cycling Power, and Cycling Speed and Cadence). ANT+ is not supported.

## Apple Watch heart rate

Install the CyComp Watch app and keep the Watch nearby. Heart rate is sent to the iPhone over Watch Connectivity. Start the workout from either the phone or the Watch.

## GPX routes

Import a `.gpx` file from the Files app, or use Open in CyComp. Select the track to show it on the map and elevation profile.
