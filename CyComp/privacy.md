CyComp does not collect personal data. Ride metrics stay on your device and, if you record a workout, in Apple Health. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

## What the app uses on your device

- **Location.** Speed, altitude, and map position while you ride. Background location runs only during a recorded workout.
- **Bluetooth.** Heart rate, power, speed, and cadence from cycling sensors you pair.
- **Health.** Reading and saving cycling workouts, including heart rate, power, cadence, speed, and distance, when you choose to record.

These permissions are used only to show and record rides. CyComp does not send this data to the developer.

## Tips

Optional tips inside the app are Apple in-app purchases. Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive your payment details. Tips do not unlock features.
